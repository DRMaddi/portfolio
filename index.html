Backend

Event-Driven Order Service

How it works: A Spring Boot microservice receives order events and processes them asynchronously through Kafka, SQS, and SNS, storing results in DynamoDB.

Goal: Keep order processing reliable and responsive even when traffic spikes or a downstream service fails.

Principles: Loose coupling, automatic retries, dead-letter queues for failed messages, and idempotent handlers so nothing is processed twice.

JavaSpring BootKafkaDynamoDB
